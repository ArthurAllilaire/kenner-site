Best AI for HVAC technicians in 2026

Most comparisons of AI for HVAC technicians are written by a vendor. This one is too: we make Kenner (getkenner.com). So for each of the seven tools below we say what it answers from, what it costs and when it is a better choice than ours, with a link to the vendor’s own page behind every claim.

ServiceTitan Atlas

What it is
The assistant built into ServiceTitan. In the field it answers from the job, customer and equipment data already in ServiceTitan, and from equipment manuals, and it helps diagnose and troubleshoot. Atlas page
Who it is for
Companies that run on ServiceTitan. Atlas is available for commercial HVAC, residential HVAC and plumbing, and only to managed technicians. Field mobile guide
Price
Not published. The equipment information and troubleshooting modules are only available with Field Pro. Use Atlas in mobile
Strengths
  • Answers from the job history, equipment, estimates and agreements already in ServiceTitan. Use Atlas in mobile
  • Looks things up in equipment manuals, spec sheets and installation guides. Atlas page
  • Diagnosis and troubleshooting help, with diagnostic tests and calculators. Atlas page
  • Finds replacement parts and adds a matched part to the invoice, the estimate or a job note. Field mobile guide
Trade-offs
  • Only available inside ServiceTitan, and in Field Pro only for managed technicians. Field mobile guide
  • Works on one make or model per question, and unsupported equipment may have no documentation. Field mobile guide
  • ServiceTitan notes that responses may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date and should be verified. Use Atlas in mobile
Pick it over Kenner if
Your company runs on ServiceTitan with Field Pro. Atlas is already in the app your technicians open, and it answers from the same job and equipment data.
Pick Kenner over it if
You are not on ServiceTitan, or you want technicians to ask by WhatsApp, iMessage or a phone call and have the whole job report written back. Atlas adds a part to the invoice or creates a job note; it does not write the report. Kenner is also not tied to the FSM: through your CRM and ERP it orders parts and prepares quotes.

Bluon MasterMechanic

What it is
Manuals, specs and parts for HVAC units by model number, plus MasterMechanic, an AI trained on 130,000 tech support calls that gives diagnostic and troubleshooting help. Bluon docs
Who it is for
HVAC contractors, residential and commercial, whose technicians meet many brands and need the data for the model in front of them. Bluon pricing
Price
Custom, set by technician count, assets and field service software. Bluon pricing
Strengths
  • HVAC-specific: specs, manuals and parts by model number, across more than 260 brands. Bluon pricing
  • Troubleshooting AI built on real support calls with Bluon’s own veteran technicians. Bluon docs
  • Embedded in field service software: Bluon lists ServiceTitan, BuildOps and Housecall Pro. Bluon pricing
  • Works in English and Spanish, and writes job summaries from the conversation. Bluon docs
Trade-offs
  • It answers from Bluon’s equipment data and support history, not from your own company’s past jobs. Bluon docs
  • HVAC and refrigeration equipment only. Bluon
  • Pricing is custom, so there is no number to compare. Bluon pricing
Pick it over Kenner if
The question is about the model, not the unit. Bluon answers from manuals, specs and parts by model number and from its support calls, and it needs none of your history.
Pick Kenner over it if
The answer is in your own past jobs, such as what fixed this fault on this unit last time. Bluon answers from its own data, not from your company’s history. The two can be used side by side.

measureQuick mQ Assist

What it is
A diagnostics app that reads Bluetooth probes and gauges in real time. mQ Assist is its AI: it talks through the system from the live readings and suggests likely causes and the measurements that would confirm them. mQ Assist page
Who it is for
Technicians who carry compatible Bluetooth tools. measureQuick positions the product for residential work. mQ Assist page
Price
The app is free. mQ Assist is part of Premier Services at $49 per user per month, month to month. measureQuick pricing
Strengths
  • Grounded in measurements, not descriptions: it works from the live readings on the screen. mQ Assist page
  • Connects to more than 80 Bluetooth tools across more than 17 brands, including Fieldpiece and Testo. measureQuick pricing
  • The core diagnostics app is free, and the paid tier has a published price. measureQuick pricing
  • Reads the data plate with the camera to identify the model, capacity and age of the system. mQ Assist page
Trade-offs
  • It needs compatible Bluetooth tools connected to the system. mQ Assist page
  • The AI features are in the paid Premier Services tier. measureQuick pricing
  • Its pages do not speak to commercial refrigeration. mQ Assist page
  • measureQuick says mQ Assist does not condemn equipment or replace your judgment. mQ Assist page
Pick it over Kenner if
You want the live readings checked. mQ Assist reasons from the Bluetooth probes on the system in front of you, the app is free and the AI is $49 per user per month.
Pick Kenner over it if
You service commercial HVAC or refrigeration and need the unit’s history and the job report. measureQuick reads the probes and Kenner reads the service record, so they answer different questions.

XOi

What it is
An equipment data platform for field service. Technicians scan a data plate to get manuals, service bulletins and the history of the unit, document the job with photos and video, and AI writes the job summary. XOi jobsite workflows
Who it is for
Companies that want every asset and every visit documented the same way, with photos, video and data plate scans. XOi jobsite workflows
Price
Not published. XOi pricing
Strengths
  • A data plate scan brings up manuals, service bulletins and equipment history on site. XOi jobsite workflows
  • AI job summaries and customer-ready reports after every visit. XOi jobsite workflows
  • An equipment library across more than 215 manufacturers. XOi
  • Guided workflows with photo and video documentation. XOi jobsite workflows
Trade-offs
  • The AI described structures data and writes summaries. XOi’s pages do not describe a conversational troubleshooting assistant. XOi jobsite workflows
  • Bought and rolled out by the company through an implementation package, not something a single technician signs up for. XOi pricing
  • Prices are not published. XOi pricing
Pick it over Kenner if
You want every asset documented with photos, video and data plate scans, and a library of manuals from more than 215 manufacturers.
Pick Kenner over it if
You want technicians to ask a question and get an answer. XOi’s pages describe summaries and structured data, not a conversational troubleshooting assistant, and Kenner needs no app.

FixBot Pro

What it is
iFixit’s AI repair assistant. For HVAC it looks up service documentation by model or fault code, builds a test sequence from the service manual, and lets a team add its own manuals and notes. FixBot Pro for HVAC
Who it is for
A technician or a small team that wants manual-based answers today, at a per-user price and with no integration project. FixBot Pro for HVAC
Price
$9 per user per month with rate limits. $30 per user per month for Professional. FixBot Pro for HVAC
Strengths
  • Every answer points back to the manufacturer literature or company material it used.
  • Says so when it needs another reading or cannot find a reliable answer.
  • Voice, photos and readings as input during the call.
  • Per-user prices are published.
FixBot Pro for HVAC
Trade-offs
  • Coverage varies by manufacturer and model.
  • OEM service manuals and uploading your own are in the Professional tier, not the $9 one.
  • It saves a service record inside FixBot, and its page names no connection to field service software, so the unit’s past jobs stay outside it unless you upload them.
FixBot Pro for HVAC
Pick it over Kenner if
You want answers from manufacturer service manuals with the source shown, for $9 to $30 per user per month and with no integration project.
Pick Kenner over it if
The unit’s past jobs matter as much as the manual. FixBot’s page names no connection to field service software, so that history stays outside it unless you upload it.

ChatGPT

What it is
OpenAI’s general-purpose chatbot. Nothing on its pages is specific to HVAC, so for a technician it is a general assistant with voice and photo input, not a trade tool.
Who it is for
Anyone who wants a free general assistant and will check what it says against the manual.
Price
Free plan. Plus is $20 per month. ChatGPT pricing
Strengths
  • A free version, with voice on web, iPhone and Android. ChatGPT Voice
  • Takes spoken questions, images and live video from the camera. ChatGPT Voice
  • Can be called on the phone: 1-800-CHATGPT from a US or Canadian number, with 30 minutes a month free. 1-800-ChatGPT
  • Can search the web and read a PDF manual you upload. Uploads are limited on the free plan. ChatGPT pricing
Trade-offs
  • OpenAI says it can produce incorrect or misleading output and advises verifying technical information. OpenAI help
  • It has no link to your company’s job history or the record of the unit in front of you. OpenAI’s connected apps page names services such as Google Drive and Slack, not field service software. Connected apps
  • It does not write the job report into your field service software.
Pick it over Kenner if
You want a free general assistant for theory, a photo of a wiring diagram or a quick sanity check, and you will verify the answer against the manual.
Pick Kenner over it if
The answer has to come from this unit’s record with the source shown, and the report has to land in your field service software. ChatGPT has no link to either.

How to choose

  • You run ServiceTitan with Field Pro: start with Atlas. It is already in the app. Add Kenner if you want questions by message or phone call and the whole report written back.
  • You are a single technician: FixBot Pro at $9 per user per month, ChatGPT’s free plan, or measureQuick’s free app. Not Kenner.
  • You are a commercial team working across many brands, with a field service system: Kenner for what happened on each unit, and Bluon alongside it for model data and parts.
  • You need live readings checked: measureQuick mQ Assist. Nothing else here reads the probes.
  • You only need manuals: Bluon for manuals and parts by model number, or FixBot Pro for manual-based answers with the source shown.
  • You want every visit documented with photos and video: XOi.
  • Your technicians would rather call than type: Kenner, which answers a phone call from your own units’ history. ChatGPT has a phone line in the US and Canada, with no link to your units.
  • You want free: ChatGPT’s free plan, or measureQuick’s app without the AI. Kenner is free while in beta, but it starts with a demo and a pilot, not a download.

Bottom line

These tools answer from different places: the field service system (Atlas), a model database (Bluon), live probes (measureQuick), captured asset records (XOi), service manuals (FixBot Pro), general knowledge (ChatGPT) and your own service history (Kenner). Pick by where your answers actually are.

If they are in your own old work orders and in the heads of a few senior technicians, that is the case we built Kenner for. If they are not, one of the other six will serve you better, and several of them you can try today.

Common questions

What is the best AI for HVAC technicians?

It depends on where the answer lives. For manuals, specs and parts by model number, Bluon. For checking live readings, measureQuick. For manual-based answers at a published price, FixBot Pro. Inside ServiceTitan, Atlas. For a commercial team that needs answers from each unit’s own history and the report written back, Kenner, which we make.

Is there a free AI for HVAC technicians?

ChatGPT has a free plan. measureQuick’s diagnostics app is free, with its AI in a paid tier at $49 per user per month. FixBot Pro starts at $9 per user per month. Kenner is free while in beta, but it starts with a demo and a pilot, not a download.

Which AI for HVAC technicians works with ServiceTitan?

Atlas is built into ServiceTitan’s mobile apps. Bluon lists ServiceTitan among the field service software it is embedded in. Kenner connects to ServiceTitan too, and technicians use it by message or phone call instead of inside the app.
