What we collect, and why

Visiting this website

When you open this website, your browser sends the data needed to load it: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser and operating system. The website is hosted by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement (docs.github.com). We do not receive or use these logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Fonts

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers and Google receives your IP address. This can include a transfer to Google LLC in the United States. We use Google Fonts so the website displays consistently; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a consistent presentation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google Fonts sets no cookies. More information: fonts.google.com/faq.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and uses no analytics or advertising tools.

Booking a call

Our booking links open a booking page in Google Calendar, part of Google Workspace, which we also use for email. Google LLC processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). If you book, we receive your name, email address and any details you enter, and use them to prepare and hold the call and to follow up afterwards. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in talking to businesses that are interested in our product (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The booking page runs on Google's website, where Google sets its own cookies and processes data for its own purposes under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

We keep your contact details and notes from our calls in our customer relationship software and may use note-taking software during calls, which we mention at the start of the call. These providers act on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR).

Contacting us

If you email or call us, we use your details to answer you and to discuss a possible collaboration with your company. Email is handled by Google LLC as our processor (Google Workspace). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and working with business customers (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where you contact us as a sole trader about a contract with you, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
